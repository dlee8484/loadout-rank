Quick verdict

ChatGPT is our provisional #1 general assistant and Claude #2. Entry prices are similar ($20/mo for ChatGPT Plus vs $17-20/mo for Claude Pro) and both have free tiers. ChatGPT is broader in consumer features; Claude includes Claude Code on Pro and above. Try both free tiers on your own tasks.

Pick ChatGPT if…

You want the broadest consumer feature set (images, voice, deep research, agents) and a cheap $8 Go tier.

Pick Claude if…
